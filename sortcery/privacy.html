Sortcery Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Applies to the Sortcery app on Android and iOS

In short: Sortcery has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is collected by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

Sortcery saves your level progress, stars, in-game currency (Glimmer), collection and settings (sound, music, haptics) on your device only. We cannot see this information, and it is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location, and the app does not use a microphone or camera.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sortcery is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or an app-specific identifier (iOS)Advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressShowing ads relevant to your region
App interactions, such as ad views and tapsAd measurement and analytics
Crash logs and performance diagnostics of the ad SDKKeeping ads working reliably

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • iOS: we request non-personalised ads only. Sortcery does not ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Android: ads may be personalised based on your advertising ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • Watching rewarded videos is always optional.

Children

Sortcery is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in Sortcery are limited to content rated PG. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share any information ourselves. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, through the AdMob SDK described above.

Security and retention

Data sent by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit. Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Google retains advertising data according to its own retention policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are handled on your device: uninstall the app to delete your game data, and use your device's ad settings to control advertising. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. For any other request or complaint, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because the app gains a new feature, we will update this page and the effective date above.
